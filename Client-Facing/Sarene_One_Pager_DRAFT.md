# Helping Organizations Turn Stated Values Into a Real Competitive Advantage

**Sarene Alsharif** — Keynote Speaker and Workshop Facilitator

Sarene Alsharif built Tad More Tailoring and Alterations around a simple, uncomfortable question: if these are truly your values, where can people actually see them in your business?
