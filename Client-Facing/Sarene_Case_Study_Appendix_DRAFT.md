## Values Matter When They Change Decisions

Sarene's experience demonstrates a central leadership challenge: translating what an organization believes into standards, practices, and decisions that can be examined and improved.

The question isn't simply whether a business has good values. It's whether those values influence how it treats people, serves customers, uses resources, and makes decisions when competing priorities arise.

### In Sarene's Words

"For us, becoming a B Corp is about accountability. This certification validates the way we already operate: putting people first, reducing waste, investing in our team, and using our business as a force for good in our community."
