## The Landing Page

**The goal:** this page exists for one job, turning someone who's already interested in you, through your content, a referral, or seeing you speak, into an actual booking inquiry. It's not for building awareness, your content and platforms handle that. It only has to work on someone who already knows who you are and is deciding whether to reach out.

**What it will contain, and why each piece is there:**
- **Who you are and what you do**, so someone lands on the page and knows in seconds whether this is relevant to them.
- **The offer and pricing, stated plainly**, no hidden pricing, no "contact us to find out," matching how you already operate.
- **Real proof**, not claims, the certifications, the numbers, the recognition that back up what you're saying.
- **Video, once real keynote or speaking footage exists.** Someone deciding whether to book a speaker wants to see you speak first, so this becomes the strongest section on the page as soon as footage is available.
- **The Values Gap Assessment**, as a lower-commitment next step for someone not ready to reach out directly yet.
- **A direct booking inquiry form.** This is the actual point of the whole page, everything above exists to get someone here.

## The Values Gap Assessment

**The goal:** give someone a genuine, useful way to check whether their own company's stated values actually show up in practice, or whether they're just words on a website, then move them toward booking your workshop.

It has two jobs, and both matter: it has to actually tell someone something true and useful about their own company, and right after, it points them toward you: if you want help closing what you just found, let's talk about bringing the workshop to your team.
