## How Content Actually Gets Made

Once the weekly shoot block is running, here's the rhythm: each session produces several raw videos, which get edited into multiple finished pieces. Combined with clips from your podcast and any keynote footage we secure, the goal is daily posting, roughly 20 to 30 video posts and a handful of other posts each month, adjusted to whatever footage is actually available that month rather than a fixed number we force.

LinkedIn gets extra attention specifically, since that's where your actual clients are and where you're already getting traction, we're aiming for daily posting there with a guaranteed floor of at least 5 posts a week even on a lighter week. Instagram is the growth engine, reaching new people. Facebook mirrors LinkedIn plus local Rockford material. YouTube is where your full, uncut talks and podcast episodes live.

Full episodes go to YouTube, shorter clips get cut for everywhere else.
