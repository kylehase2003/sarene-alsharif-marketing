# A Comprehensive Strategic Plan for Building the Personal Brand of "Sereen"

**Founder and CEO of Tad More Tailoring, A Proven Field Authority in Humane Leadership and Corporate Sustainability**

---

## Executive Summary (Updated)

This piece of information changes the very core of the entire plan, which is why we have reworked it, not as a side addition, but as a new spine that runs through all six of its pillars.

**The decisive strategic difference:** In the first version of the plan, Sereen's authority was built on "trusted theoretical knowledge and expertise" (a Master's degree + TEDx). Now, her authority is built on "living, auditable proof": she didn't just explain why compassionate leadership and sustainability succeed commercially, she built a real, officially certified B Corp, profitable company, on exactly these principles. This elevates her from the category of "trusted advisor" to a rarer and commercially more valuable category: **"The Practitioner Who Did It."**

### The New Four-Pillar Authority Stack

Every appearance, post, or presentation by Sereen from now on must invoke, even if only implicitly, these four layers together:

1. **The Science** (why the idea works): A Master's in Public Health (MPH) as an analytical framework.
2. **Field Proof** (how it actually works): Tad More Tailoring, a real company, officially B Corp certified, operating on a sustainable mail-in tailoring and clothing-alteration model, achieving real growth and profitability.
3. **Institutional Depth** (why her methodology can be trusted more broadly): The Tad More Sustainability Institute, an educational/research institution founded by Sereen, which elevates her from "owner of a successful company" to "builder of a body of knowledge" in sustainability.
4. **Public Voice** (how the idea reaches the audience): Her TEDx appearance as a narrative and media asset.

This four-part framework is the tool that will be used to filter every subsequent content or marketing decision: any idea that doesn't rest on at least one of these four pillars will be reworked or dropped.

With Tad More Tailoring entering as a primary pillar, the order of priority in any bio or introduction changes.

## Axis 2: Event and Conference Strategy, Redirected

### 2.1 Reclassifying Her Value as a Speaker (and Justifying a Fee Increase)

Having a real, certified B Corp company reclassifies Sereen from a "general leadership speaker" into a **"proof-of-concept speaker"**: a category that commands noticeably higher fees in the conference market, because organizers of B Corp and sustainability conferences specifically are actively searching for speakers with a genuine operational story, not just theoretical frameworks.

**Pricing recommendation based on event type:**

- **B Corp / Sustainability Summits (highest price tier):** Here Sereen is presented primarily as a B Corp founder, with leadership consulting as an additional layer, the fit is nearly perfect, justifying the highest possible fee.
- **HR and general leadership conferences (standard-to-premium tier):** Presented as a compassionate-leadership expert, with the Tad More story used as supporting proof rather than the main topic.
- **General entrepreneurship forums (standard tier):** The "from a small shop to a globally certified brand" angle, framed as an inspiring growth story.

### 2.2 New Talk Titles (Replacing or Added to Previous Ones)

- "How I Turned a Small Tailoring Shop into a Profitable B Corp, and a Lesson for Every Business Leader"
- "The Circular Economy Isn't a Marketing Luxury: Real Numbers From My Company on Zero Waste as a Profitable Model"
- "The B Corp Certification Journey From the Inside: What No One Tells You About the Real Challenges"
- "Compassionate Leadership in a Real Workshop: How I Treated My Tailoring Team as a Strategic Asset, Not an Operating Cost"

### 2.3 The Updated Speaker Kit (Speaker Kit 2.0)

A new document is added to the kit alongside the personal bio: a **"Company One-Pager"** that summarizes Tad More Tailoring in the language of numbers and results (the business model, the date of B Corp certification, growth and loyalty indicators without needing sensitive financial figures, a general trend such as "growth in customer loyalty" or "decline in staff turnover" is enough). This document becomes the "proof sheet" that conference organizers attach to the event's promotional materials.

### 2.4 Printed Materials, Brochures Now Built on Real, Not Hypothetical, Proof

The diagnostic brochure (from the previous version) is rebuilt with one essential addition: a **"Field-Tested"** stamp.

- After the five-question diagnostic, a standing line is added: *"These aren't theoretical recommendations, this is the exact same strategy I applied myself at my own company, Tad More Tailoring, and it led to measurable growth in our customer loyalty and profits."*
- In consulting proposals sent to companies, a **"Case Study Appendix"** is added: a fixed, one-page piece built on the Tad More Tailoring journey (traditional tailoring operation) → the decision (embracing sustainability and compassionate leadership despite the upfront cost) → the real obstacles faced during B Corp certification → the outcome (growth in loyalty and profitability). This appendix replaces any generic "client testimonials," because it's far stronger: it's the founder's own testimony.

## Axis 3: Lifestyle and Practical Embodiment of Values, Completely Reframed

The previous version of this pillar was built on "Sereen's personal life" in isolation from any specific workplace. Now, we have a real, tangible stage: the Tad More Tailoring workshop itself. This shifts all lifestyle content from "general statements" to "documented reality", the most important difference between content that looks like marketing and content that feels genuine.

### 3.1 Human Interaction Inside the Workshop (Not an Abstract "Workplace")

- Documented content (monthly) from inside the Tad More workshop: a moment of appreciation for a tailor, a celebration of a team achievement, or a simple everyday scene that reflects respect for the tailors' manual skill as a "human asset," not "labor."
- The implicit message: "Before I advise any company on how to treat its employees, I practice it every day with my own team in the workshop."

### 3.2 From Operational to Personal Sustainability (Zero Waste & Circular Fashion)

This is the new golden thread that connects Sereen's professional work to her personal life with complete fluidity:

- **Circular fashion as a personal lifestyle:** content documenting how Sereen applies the "Repair, Don't Replace" principle to her own and her family's wardrobe, a natural and highly convincing extension of her company's work in altering and repairing clothes.
- **A zero-waste kitchen/home:** connecting corporate Zero Waste concepts to tangible household practices (reducing food waste, reuse), tied back to her public-health background (healthy nutrition + sustainability as complementary, not separate, concepts).

### 3.3 Raising Children on Conscious Consumption, Instead of a Generic "Instilling Values"

Instead of the previous generic framing, it's replaced with a specific, strongly narrative angle:

- **Sample phrasing:** *"I taught my kids to mend a worn-out shirt instead of throwing it away, exactly what our company does with thousands of garments every month. Same lesson: everything deserves a second chance before it's discarded."*
- The same caution remains in place (once every 4–6 weeks, no sensitive details or clear photos of the children), but now every story is tied directly to the core of the company's work, making it more consistent and professional rather than a purely personal "aside."

### 3.4 The Updated Balance (80/20)

The principle of 80% leadership/thought-leadership content and 20% human content stays as is, but that same 20% is now built entirely on real scenes from Tad More Tailoring and a life organically connected to it, not a parallel life separate from the work. This fully unifies the message:

> "Sereen doesn't live two separate lives (advisor on one hand, mother/human being on the other), she lives a single value, embodied in every detail."
