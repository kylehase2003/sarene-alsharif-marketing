# Operational Customer Journey & Funnel (Internal)

This is the working version of the plan, for us, not Sarene. It answers one question: how do we actually get from where we are now to a signed booking, month by month, and why does each month's work make the next one possible.

Every deliverable named in this document is broken into its actual required parts, pulled directly from `Client-Facing/Sarene_Alsharif_Service_Contract.md` (what we're contractually required to deliver) and `Client-Facing/Sarene_Alsharif_Strategy_File.md` (what the strategy says those deliverables need to contain). A deliverable only counts as done when every part listed under it is done, not when the label has been touched. "Landing page" is not one task, it's six specific things living on one page. The same logic applies to every composite item below.

The funnel stages still matter, Awareness, Interest, Consideration, Decision, Conversion, but they're not the organizing structure here. This document is laid out by month so it reads as a sequence of cause and effect. A full index at the bottom cross-checks every single item from the contract and the strategy file against where it lives in this plan, so nothing gets missed and nothing gets marked done by accident.

## Month 1: Give the funnel somewhere to send a serious lead

**The actual problem this month solves:** right now, if someone saw Sarene speak, or saw a post, and got genuinely curious, there's almost nothing to hand them. Interest with nowhere to go just evaporates. That's the leak this month closes.

**Note on scope:** per the contract (Section 2), full monthly deliverable targets apply from Month 2 onward. Month 1 deliverables are the reduced, written-agreement scope below, reflecting the Onboarding Period (first 10 business days: present prototypes, get written approval before full production begins).

**One-Pager.** Not done until all five parts exist: name, the two offerings with one-line descriptions, the three pricing tiers, five proof points (B Corp score 115.0 vs. 50.9 median, the Department of Labor apprenticeship, 35,000+ orders, the two named awards, press coverage), and contact information. Status: in progress.

**Case Study Appendix.** Not done until all five parts exist: the catalyst, the decision to certify despite the cost, what the audit actually required, the score, and the result. Status: content complete, ready to use.

**Bio rewrite.** Not done until it follows the confirmed order: what she does now (speaker/facilitator), the proof (B Corp, Tad More), the platform (TEDx), the credential (MPH, LDN). Status: in progress.

**Brand guidelines (one-time contract deliverable).** Partial info received directly from her (2026-10-09): title font Benedict, subheadline font Proxima Nova, a six-color palette. This is from her old brand guidelines though, same risk as the outdated speaker sheet, needs confirmation it still matches the current positioning before it's built into anything. Still needs an owner.

**Account setup and optimization (one-time contract deliverable, per platform):**
- Instagram: already established, 1,400+ existing posts stay as-is, this platform needs positioning/bio alignment only, not a fresh setup. Role: growth engine, reach and discovery beyond her existing network.
- LinkedIn: in progress, being treated as co-primary. Role: where she already has traction, gets the heaviest production focus, leads with the business-case content.
- YouTube: in progress, new channel being created. Role: home for full, uncut keynote and podcast footage, the flagship credibility asset a conference organizer would watch before booking.
- Facebook: not addressed anywhere yet. Needs a decision on whether it's a fresh setup or an existing page being repositioned. Role: secondary distribution of the same content, plus local Rockford community material.
- TikTok: not started. Resharing-only per strategy, but the account itself still needs to exist and be optimized before anything can be reshared to it. Role: reshare destination only, nothing produced specifically for it.

**Recordings (strategy blocker, Note 17).** Not done until recordings are confirmed to actually show her speaking, a batch was received, still ambiguous. Status: open, waiting on Sarene.

**Weekly shoot day (strategy blocker, Note 20).** Resolved, every Friday.

**Podcast co-host/guest decision (strategy blocker, Note 19).** Not a default, needs a real decision before any podcast production planning. Status: open, waiting on Sarene.

**Booking outreach ownership.** Flagged as a real operational gap in both the strategy file and the contract's "Full team direction" scope item. Needs a confirmed owner this month, bookings are the one thing in this plan that produces revenue, so this doesn't stay unowned past Month 1.

**Why this has to come first:** none of Month 2's volume matters if a curious lead still has nothing real to evaluate once they show up.

**What "done" looks like by the end of this month:** one-pager, case study, and bio finished and usable in a real conversation. Brand guidelines started. Every platform account exists and is positioned, not just the ones that were easy. Recordings resolved (shoot day already is, every Friday). Booking outreach has a named owner.

## Month 2: Turn strangers into known, identified leads

**The actual problem this month solves:** Month 1 builds the material, but material sitting unused doesn't create leads. There's no mechanism yet that turns a stranger into a named contact we can follow up with. That mechanism is what this month builds. Full contractual monthly targets now apply.

**Values Gap Assessment / lead magnet.** Not done until all of these exist: the questions themselves (being drafted now), a decided hosting tool (Typeform, Google Form, or built into the landing page, not yet decided), the CTA wired into the workshop conversation, and a way for every completed quiz to land somewhere we can actually see and act on it. Status: named and purpose defined, content in progress, hosting and capture mechanism both still open.

**Landing page.** Not done until all six parts are live on one page: who she is, her offer and pricing, her proof points, the speaker reel, the Values Gap Assessment, and a direct booking inquiry form that checks date and availability. Status: not started, blocked on the reel and the quiz both existing first.

**Design refinements from reviewing real speaker landing pages (2026-10-12), Codie Sanchez, Myron Golden, Carla Harris:** "who she is" should include a pull-quote from her own thesis, not just paragraph bio, since we only have one third-party testimonial. "Proof points" should render press coverage as an actual logo strip, not a text list. One single dominant CTA (the booking form) throughout the page, not a competing button at the end of every section, that was the clearest mistake across the pages reviewed.

**Speaker reel.** Not done until it's actually cut from confirmed, in-hand recordings of her speaking. Status: blocked on Month 1's recordings question.

**Full marketing funnel (one-time contract deliverable).** This is the deliverable that this entire document exists to satisfy. It is not done until Awareness, Interest, Consideration, and Decision are all live and actually connected to each other, meaning the landing page and quiz are built, not just planned. Status: in progress, this document and the client-facing funnel deck are the planning layer, the live mechanism isn't built yet.

**Full content production across the nine core content types and four personal categories** (Proof Over Promise, Where the Belief Came From, Built Around People, Second Life Public, Second Life Personal, The Five Places Values Actually Show Up, The Business Case, Ask Sarene, Confidence Shows Up as Performance, plus the four personal categories). Status: test batch drafted for a handful, full-scale production not yet running.

**Content formats, applied across those types:** Talking-Head Take (straight to camera, one point, fits types 6, 7, 9), Story-Time (a single personal experience, fits types 2, 3, 5), Risky Text (quote something real, react on screen, fits type 1), Rating and Ranking (real workplace systems or behaviors rated fast and opinionated, fits types 1, 6), Ask Sarene (real questions answered unscripted, its own type and format at once), Voiceover (held for later, reserved for more motivational material once core formats are running consistently). Real keynote/conference footage comes first whenever it exists, genuine unedited to-camera content fills every other gap.

**Recurring monthly deliverables, now at full contract scope:**
- 20 to 30 edited videos (shoot day locked, every Friday, production can now ramp).
- Minimum 4 designed posts.
- 4 blog posts, English, optimized for search. Not started anywhere, and no publishing destination has been confirmed yet, likely her WordPress site once the business email/domain setup finishes, but this hasn't been explicitly decided.
- LinkedIn: daily targeted, minimum 5 a week.
- TikTok: resharing of existing content.
- 1 content calendar, delivered before the month starts. Not yet produced for any month so far.
- 1 monthly social media plan. The strategy file functions as the master plan, but a month-specific plan hasn't been produced as its own deliverable.
- 1 monthly performance report, due by the 5th of the following month. Not yet produced, no live metrics exist yet to report on.

**Active outreach to HR/leadership conferences and corporate contacts begins.** Needs the owner confirmed in Month 1 actually running it.

**Why this has to come second:** this is the month the funnel stops being one-directional content and starts producing something we can act on.

**What "done" looks like by the end of this month:** the quiz and landing page are live, not planned. A real list of quiz completions and inquiries exists. All four recurring content deliverables (calendar, social plan, report, blog posts) have actually been produced at least once, not just targeted. Outreach has gone out to a defined list.

## Month 3: Convert activity into an actual signed booking

**The actual problem this month solves:** by now there's volume and there are leads, but volume and leads aren't revenue. This month is about closing.

**What gets done:**
- Every outreach conversation includes the case study appendix, and the speaker reel if ready.
- The buyer definition gets narrowed using real response data from Month 2, not guesswork.
- Fast follow-up discipline on every inquiry, the point where a lead is most likely to be lost through slowness, not lack of interest.
- All recurring monthly deliverables continue (calendar, social plan, report, blog posts, video/post counts, LinkedIn cadence).

**Why this comes last:** closing only works once there's something real to close.

**What "done" looks like by the end of this month:** at least one new, paid booking signed. Not content volume, a result.

## The Loop

A signed booking isn't the end of the chain, it's new fuel for the start of it. If the event gets recorded, that footage becomes the next speaker reel update and the next round of proof content, feeding straight back into Month 1's material. The plan compounds if we keep closing this loop deliberately.
