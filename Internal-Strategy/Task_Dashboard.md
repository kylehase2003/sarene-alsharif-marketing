## Content Idea Bank

Pull from here whenever you need a post idea. Each one maps to a content type from the strategy.

**Core lane (80%)**
1. Proof Over Promise — a real company whose values visibly show up in what they do, or something she personally witnessed that impressed her.
2. Where the Belief Came From — the Syria repair-culture contrast, the decision to start Tad More, an early mistake that reshaped her thinking.
3. Built Around People — Mazen isn't a natural teacher, Noor and Adriana are, training built around that.
4. Second Life, Public — Restyle the Runway, Green Threads, framed as a real ongoing partnership, not a one-off.
5. Second Life, Personal — the wedding dress stories, the christening gown, any new customer story.
6. The Five Places Values Actually Show Up — environment, people, community, customers, how the business is run, doing it right versus hollow.
7. The Business Case — retention numbers, the Texas customer loyalty example, decision consistency.
8. Ask Sarene — answer a real question directly, unscripted.
9. Confidence Shows Up as Performance — "if you look good, you make more money," physical discomfort draining focus, presentation tied to leadership presence. Supporting only, low frequency.

**Personal (20%)**
1. Workshop and team moments — shown, not told.
2. Fundraisers and community involvement — same real events, light, no lesson attached.
3. Fitness and personal interests — century bike ride training, art, coffee, orchids.
4. Personal achievements — e.g. her Color Analysis certification, no business angle needed.

**Formats, match to type**
- Talking-Head Take → types 6, 7, 9
- Story-Time → types 2, 3, 5
- Risky Text → type 1
- Rating and Ranking → types 1, 6
- Ask Sarene → its own type and format
- Voiceover → held for later, not active yet

Real keynote/conference footage always comes first when it exists. Genuine unedited to-camera content fills everything else.
