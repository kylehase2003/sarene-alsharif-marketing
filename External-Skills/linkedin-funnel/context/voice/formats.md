# Formats

| Format | When to use it | Reference | What the asset needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Talking-Head Take | Straight to camera, one clear point, no gimmick. Fits pillar 1 content types 6, 7, and 9. | Master Reference, Content Formats | Weekly shoot day footage |
| Story-Time | A single personal experience, told start to finish. Fits pillar 2/3 content types 2, 3, and 5. | Master Reference, Content Formats | Weekly shoot day footage |
| Risky Text | Quote something real, then react to it directly on screen. Fits pillar 1 content type 1. | Master Reference, Content Formats | A real quote/source plus on-camera reaction |
| Rating/Ranking, 1 through 10 | Real workplace systems, behaviors, or stated values, rated fast and opinionated. A punchier delivery of types 1 and 6. | Master Reference, Content Formats | Weekly shoot day footage |
| Ask Sereen | Real questions, answered directly and unscripted. Its own type and format at once. | Master Reference, Content Formats | Sourced real questions, not invented ones |
| Voiceover Content | Held for later, narrating over real b-roll only, no still images. Reserved for more motivational material once core formats are running consistently. | Master Reference, Content Formats | Real b-roll, not yet in production |
| Priority footage, real keynote/conference clips | Comes first whenever it exists, the single strongest trust-builder, proof she's an actual, working, paid speaker. | Master Reference, Content Formats | Secured recordings, currently blocked on getting her existing talk recordings, edit note 17 |
