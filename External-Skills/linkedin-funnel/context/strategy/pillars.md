# Pillars

Derived from the project's already-finalized 9-type, 80/20 content plan, not a fresh pillar exercise. These 4 pillars group the 9 established content types; the type numbers below are the source project's own numbering (Master Reference, 80/20 Content Plan).

## Pillar decisions

| Slot | Name | Promise | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Proof, Not Promises | Show whether a value actually shows up in a decision, hers or another real company's, not whether it's stated well. Types 1 (Proof Over Promise), 6 (Five Places Values Show Up), 7 (The Business Case). | Type 1 needs a real, verified example from her own network, nothing gets named without her confirming it firsthand. Not currently in production. |
| 2 | Built From Real Experience | Explain why she believes what she believes and how she actually runs her team, through her own lived experience, not theory. Types 2 (Where the Belief Came From), 3 (Built Around People). | The TEDx rejection-count story stays private. The Maen/Syria detail stays specific, no generalizing into a refugee narrative. |
| 3 | Real Relationships | Prove community and customer investment is real and ongoing, not a one-time gesture. Types 4 (Second Life, Public), 5 (Second Life, Personal). | Community content stays light and un-argued when it's the 20% version (fundraisers, team moments), no lesson attached. |
| 4 | Direct and Personal | Build the sense of a real person behind the business: answering real questions, occasional confidence/presentation content, and genuine personal texture. Types 8 (Ask Sereen), 9 (Confidence Shows Up as Performance, low-frequency, supporting only), plus the full 20% personal lane (team moments, fundraisers, fitness/interests, personal achievements). | Type 9 and Power Look content stay occasional and supporting, never a second keynote topic. |

## Topic structure

Not using the default 4-topics-per-pillar/4-subtopics scaffold, the 9 content types (with their own defined angles, already specified in the Master Reference's 80/20 Content Plan) are the actual topic layer. No Notion board is connected yet, this stays as a reference table here rather than a populated Notion tree.

## Funnel fit and weekly mix

- **Top of funnel:** Pillar 1 (Proof, Not Promises) does the heaviest lifting, it's the core thesis and most shareable framework content. Pillar 4's Ask Sereen is also top-of-funnel, self-generating and accessible.
- **Middle of funnel:** Pillar 2 (Built From Real Experience) builds trust in her specifically as a credible, practiced source, not just a framework.
- **Bottom of funnel:** Pillar 1's Business Case content (type 7) and Pillar 3's proof pieces support the direct booking case, alongside the dedicated sales materials (one-pager, case study appendix, Values Gap Self-Assessment).
- **Weekly mix:** 80% of posted content across Pillars 1 to 3 combined (the core lane), 20% Pillar 4's personal-texture content, per the project's already-set 80/20 split. Exact weekly count depends on the weekly shoot day's output, see `strategy/goals.md`.
