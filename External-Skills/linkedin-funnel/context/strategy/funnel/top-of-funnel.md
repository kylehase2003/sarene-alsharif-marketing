# Top of funnel

## Audience to reach

Business leaders, HR and people-function professionals, conference organizers, on LinkedIn and Instagram primarily. See `audience/ideal-follower.md` for the fuller profile and the still-open follower-vs-buyer distinction.

## Topics and formats

Pillar 1 content (Proof, Not Promises) and Ask Sereen (Pillar 4) do the most top-of-funnel work, they're the most shareable, framework-driven pieces. Primary format is the Talking-Head Take (straight to camera, one clear point), which the project's format guide maps specifically to types 6, 7, and 9.

## What to measure

Not yet formalized for LinkedIn specifically. Overall program goal is at least 1 new booking by Month 3, see `strategy/goals.md`.
