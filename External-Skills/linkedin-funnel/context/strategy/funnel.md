# Funnel

## What each stage does

- Top: earn attention from the intended audience.
- Middle: explain the method and build trust.
- Bottom: help a ready reader take a defined next step.

## Conversion target

A signed booking agreement, one of three fixed offerings: Keynote ($7,000), Workshop ($5,000), or Keynote + Workshop ($10,000). No ongoing consulting, no open-ended retainer with Sarene herself, that's a settled critical fact for this project, not something to reintroduce.

## Inbound response

Not yet formalized. The lead magnet ("The Values Gap Self-Assessment") is the designed on-ramp: a company leader checks whether their own stated values actually show up in practice, then gets a direct call to action to talk about bringing the workshop to their team. This tool itself hasn't been written yet, blocked on overall strategy approval.

## Signal-based outbound

Deliberately not part of this workspace. The 4-skill OXYGEN cluster from the LinkedIn Funnel template (Profile Watcher, ICP qualification, sequencer, unibox) was excluded on install per direct instruction, this workspace covers content and strategy only, not outreach/growth automation. See `../../LinkedIn_Funnel_Skill.md` for what was excluded and why.

## Measurement

Not yet formalized beyond the overall goal of at least 1 new booking by Month 3 of the 90-day plan. No dashboard or reporting cadence has been set for LinkedIn specifically yet.
