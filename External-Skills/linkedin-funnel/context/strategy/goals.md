## Cadence

Daily posting is the overall target across platforms, fed by three sources: a weekly shoot day (her own on-camera time), podcast clips (once the podcast launches, 4 episodes a month), and any secured keynote/conference footage. On LinkedIn specifically, the guaranteed floor is 5 posts a week even if daily isn't hit every week.

## Platforms

Instagram and LinkedIn are co-primary. Instagram is the growth engine (reach and discovery). LinkedIn is where the strategy doubles down, she's already active there with real traction, so it gets the heavier production focus and leads with the 80% business-case content. Facebook is secondary distribution. YouTube is the flagship credibility asset, home for full uncut keynote/conference footage and full podcast episodes. TikTok is resharing only, nothing produced specifically for it.
