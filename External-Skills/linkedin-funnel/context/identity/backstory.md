## Turning points

- Meeting Maen: a skilled tailor with no shop to use it in, met while helping Syrian refugees. This is the specific, named catalyst for Tad More, not a generalized refugee story.
- Identifying the actual mechanism behind fast fashion waste: people don't repair clothes mainly because they lack access to a good tailor, not because they don't care. This reframes the problem from a values gap to an access gap, which is what she built the business to close.
- Putting her own company through an actual B Corp certification, an outside audit scored against all five dimensions (not just environmental), rather than just claiming values on a mission page. Certification journey itself (catalyst, cost, what the audit required) has not yet been publicly told, still needs to be captured from her directly.

## Stories to tell
