# Mission

## Who to help

Organizations that want to understand values as a competitive business advantage, not just an ethical stance: conferences, corporate leadership teams, HR and people-leadership functions especially, and professional and trade organizations. Broader than the uniform/apparel niche her first major booking (NAUMD) came from.

## Change to create

Move companies from having values to operating by them. Not adding more values-messaging, closing the gap between what a company claims and what it actually does, in hiring, training, client relationships, community involvement, and how the business is run day to day.

## Why this matters

In her own words: "building a values-based business isn't simply an ethical decision. It can be a competitive business strategy." The gap between stated and operating values isn't just a moral inconsistency, it's an expensive one, lost retention, lost trust, lost differentiation. She built Tad More to answer that question about her own company first, through an actual third-party B Corp audit, not a claim.
