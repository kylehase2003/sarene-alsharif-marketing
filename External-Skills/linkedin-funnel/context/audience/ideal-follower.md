# Ideal follower

## Who the content is for

Business leaders and HR/people functions at companies, conferences, and professional or trade organizations, the buyer-adjacent audience the content plan is built around. Events focused on HR or leadership are called out specifically as an especially strong fit.

## Their situation and problem

They lead or influence an organization that has stated values (a mission statement, a set of "core values" on a website) but can't clearly point to where those values actually show up in day-to-day decisions, hiring, training, how mistakes get handled, where money gets spent. That gap is costing them, in retention, in customer trust, in differentiation from competitors saying the same things without proof.

## Their own words

Not directly captured yet, we don't have verified quotes from her actual audience/buyers describing their problem in their own language. Flagged as an open gap, worth capturing from her booking conversations or workshop intake once those exist.

## What they want

A way to close the gap between stated and operating values that's practical, not just inspirational, the workshop is specifically built to produce identified gaps and concrete actions, not just motivation. Proof that this works from someone who's actually done it, not a theorist.

## What they object to

Generic "sustainability" or ESG messaging that feels like marketing rather than substance, greenwashing skepticism is described as a live, current conversation. Being sold consulting or ongoing services when what they actually want is a contained keynote/workshop engagement with tools they keep afterward.

## Difference from the product buyer

Distinction not fully captured yet. What we do know: the actual booking buyer is a conference organizer, HR leader, or leadership team with budget authority ($7,000 to $10,000 range). The broader LinkedIn/Instagram follower audience is likely wider than just active buyers, HR and culture-focused professionals who engage with the content but aren't personally booking a keynote. Worth explicitly separating "who reads this content" from "who signs a booking agreement" once there's real engagement data to look at.
